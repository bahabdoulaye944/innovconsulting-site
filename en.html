Our own case

We apply it to ourselves first.

Nimba Eat, our food delivery app in Conakry, is our testing ground. Riders without smartphones, cash payments, network outages: we automate under the same constraints as you.

  • Live

    Orders passed on with no middleman

    The customer orders in the app and the restaurant receives and confirms it directly. Nobody re-types anything.

  • Live

    Code-based tracking, no data entry

    One code at restaurant pickup, another at delivery: every step is recorded automatically.

  • In progress

    Trips assigned by automated call

    The rider gets a call, presses 1 if available, then receives the trip by SMS. No smartphone needed.

  • In progress

    Payments reconciled by an AI agent

    Cash and Orange Money matched against orders; only discrepancies reach the manager.
